# Tabel Klaim: perbedaan-cpns-dan-pppk

Disetujui owner: 5 November 2026

Sources were read on 5 November 2026. For a regulation page on `peraturan.bpk.go.id`, `peraturan.go.id`, or `jdih.kemenkeu.go.id`, the location points into the regulation text linked from that page. PP 11/2017 and PP 49/2018 implement UU 5/2014 and stay in force through Pasal 75 UU 20/2023; JDIH BPK records PP 17/2020 as the only amendment of PP 11/2017 and none for PP 49/2018. PP 17/2020 adds Pasal 34A to PP 11/2017: when prajabatan cannot be held within the masa percobaan, the Calon PNS is appointed after passing it; the 1-year masa percobaan in Pasal 34 is unchanged. Pasal 5 PP 7/1977 is not changed by any of its 19 amendments listed on JDIH BPK. JDIH BPK lists no 2026 PP on jaminan pensiun Pegawai ASN as of 5 November 2026.
