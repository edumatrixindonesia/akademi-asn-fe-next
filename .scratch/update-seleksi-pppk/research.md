# Research: Update Informasi Seleksi PPPK 2026

Researched on 2026-10-10. Companion to `.scratch/update-seleksi-cpns/research.md`, which already covers PermenPANRB 6/2024 being in force, SSCASN as the only portal, free registration, and the BKN anti-hoax statements of Jan and Jun 2026. Those facts are not re-verified here.

Source tiers used:

- **Tier A**: KemenPANRB (menpan.go.id, jdih.menpan.go.id, data-jdih.menpan.go.id), BKN (bkn.go.id and subdomains), SSCASN, regulation text.
- **Tier B**: the opening agency's own .go.id announcement, used only for that agency's formasi and schedule (Kemensos, Kemdiktisaintek, KemenHAM).
- **Tier C**: Kemendikdasmen official pages (kemendikdasmen.go.id and its subdomains), used only for teacher policy.

Every quote below was read on the fetched page or PDF itself. Scanned PDFs were read as page images. Each such case is marked "(scanned)" together with its text corroboration, if any.

## Status per 2026-10-10

- **No general or national PPPK 2026 selection has been announced.**
  - SSCASN shows only "Seleksi Sekolah Kedinasan 2026", and its Dasar Hukum page is empty.
  - No KepmenPANRB setting national kebutuhan PPPK TA 2026 was found.
- **Instansi-level PPPK selections did run in calendar 2026.** All registration went through SSCASN and testing used CAT BKN. All three below are past the testing stage.
  1. **PPPK Guru SMA Unggul Garuda Baru (Kemdiktisaintek), TA 2026: 96 formasi.** KepmenPANRB 43/2026. Registration ran 2–16 Feb 2026 and results were announced 25 Apr 2026. **Completed.**
  2. **PPPK Guru dan Tenaga Kependidikan Sekolah Rakyat (Kemensos), TA 2026: 8.180 formasi (3.053 guru + 5.127 tendik).** KepmenPANRB 215/2026. Registration ran 8–14 Jun 2026, CAT ran 17–31 Jul 2026 at 42 sites, and 118.432 participants sat the test. The Seleksi Kompetensi results were published 6 Aug 2026. The final integration results (Sep 2026) were seen only in non-primary reproductions; see Unverified.
  3. **PPPK KemenHAM: TA 2025, 500 formasi, run Jan–May 2026.** KepmenPANRB 1307/2025. The final post-sanggah result has been published. **Completed.** It is labelled "Tahun 2025" by KemenHAM, although BKN calls it "Kemenham 2026".
- **PPPK paruh waktu**
  - Originally governed by KepmenPANRB 16/2025 (13 Jan 2025).
  - Now also governed by **PermenPANRB 9/2026** (enacted 19 Jun 2026, promulgated 26 Jun 2026). It lets the PPK propose conversion to PPPK "berdasarkan pertimbangan ketersediaan anggaran dan hasil penilaian/evaluasi kinerja".
  - For **teachers**, Kemendikdasmen (Tier C) states that guru PPPK paruh waktu become PPPK penuh waktu **from 1 Jan 2027, automatically, without selection**. No KemenPANRB or BKN primary page confirming that date was found.
  - Follow-up 2026-10-13 (§3): still no new regulation. BKN, as quoted in news only, frames the 2027 conversion as the existing Pasal 24–25 mechanism: no test, but it still needs formasi, a PPK proposal, and BKN review.
- **Most recent national PPPK test format: KepmenPANRB 347/2024** (with 348/2024 for guru and 349/2024 for nakes). BKN still cited 347/2024 as the basis for the Sekolah Rakyat CAT on 30 Jun 2026.
  - All of the site's PPPK 2024 numbers match the decree text, with the caveats listed in §5.

## 2. KepmenPANRB setting kebutuhan PPPK TA 2026

**No national decree was found.** Two instansi-specific decrees exist. Both are known only from the opening agencies' announcements; the decree texts were not found on JDIH.

- **KepmenPANRB 215/2026** (20 Mei 2026): kebutuhan PPPK tingkat instansi, Kemensos 2026. Cited in Kemensos 1995/2026 (§1a), Tier B citation.
- **KepmenPANRB 43/2026**: kebutuhan PPPK tingkat instansi, Kemdiktisaintek. Cited in Kemdiktisaintek 0065/2026 (§1b), Tier B citation.
- **Not found on JDIH.** The guessed file paths data-jdih.menpan.go.id/dokumen/2026kepmenpanrb215.pdf and …043.pdf returned 404, and the JDIH search is behind reCAPTCHA.
- **Legal mechanism.** [PermenPANRB 6/2024 Pasal 6 ayat (6)](https://www.bkn.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2024permenpanrb-Pengadaan-CASN-.pdf), via the CPNS file: kebutuhan ASN is set "dengan Keputusan Menteri". Without a national KepmenPANRB for TA 2026 there is no official national PPPK 2026 formasi count.

## 3. PPPK paruh waktu

- **KepmenPANRB 16/2025 tentang PPPK Paruh Waktu, enacted 13 Jan 2025, signed by Rini Widyantini.** [PDF](https://data-jdih.menpan.go.id/dokumen/2025kepmenpanrb016.pdf), Tier A (scanned; read as page images).
  - Diktum PERTAMA: "Pegawai Pemerintah dengan Perjanjian Kerja (PPPK) Paruh Waktu adalah pegawai aparatur sipil negara (ASN) yang diangkat berdasarkan perjanjian kerja dan diberikan upah sesuai dengan ketersediaan anggaran instansi pemerintah."
  - Diktum KEEMPAT: "Pengadaan PPPK Paruh Waktu dilakukan berdasarkan hasil seleksi ASN tahun anggaran 2024."

- **Follow-up 2026-10-13: is there a newer regulation for the teacher conversion?**
  - **No new KemenPANRB regulation was found.**
    - PermenPANRB 2026 No. 10–16 and No. 19 on data-jdih.menpan.go.id cover other subjects: jabatan fungsional, tunjangan kinerja, and sekolah kedinasan (No. 13). None of them covers PPPK paruh waktu.

    - menpan.go.id still fails on TLS, so KemenPANRB news after 3 Sep 2026 remains unchecked.
  - **"Tanpa seleksi" means the Pasal 24–25 mechanism, not a new automatic rule.** Kepala Biro Humas BKN Wisudo Putro Nugroho, 21 Sep 2026, quoted by [JPNN](https://www.jpnn.com/news/pembahasan-rapbn-2027-alokasi-gaji-tunjangan-pppk-sudah-disetujui-dpr): "Dalam PermenPANRB 9 Tahun 2026, apabila anggarannya tersedia dan formasinya sudah ditetapkan, pejabat pembina kepegawaian (PPK) bisa langsung mengusulkan perubahan status kepada kepala BKN untuk ditetapkan."

    - Read this way, the Kemendikdasmen claim and the regulation do not contradict each other. "Otomatis tanpa seleksi" means no test. The status change still needs the Menteri's formasi, a PPK proposal, and BKN's pertimbangan teknis.
  - **Budget condition.** DPR approved Rp23,0 triliun in RAPBN 2027 for the teacher conversion on 24 Sep 2026. This was reported in news and by Kemendikdasmen (Tier C), not by Kemenkeu.

Source: [PermenPANRB 6/2024 PDF hosted by BKN](https://www.bkn.go.id/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2024permenpanrb-Pengadaan-CASN-.pdf), enacted 18 Jul 2024, Tier A (text PDF).

### Verification of the site's PPPK 2024 numbers (all against KepmenPANRB 347/2024)

| Site claim | Decree text | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 145 soal | Diktum KEDUA PULUH DUA: "adalah 145 (seratus empat puluh lima) butir soal, dengan rincian: a. … teknis sejumlah 90 …; b. … manajerial sejumlah 25 …; c. … sosial kultural sejumlah 20 …; dan d. wawancara sejumlah 10 (sepuluh) butir soal." | Match. **Caveat:** the 145 includes the 10 wawancara items. 90 + 25 + 20 = 135, plus 10 = 145. The site must not present 145 as teknis + manajerial + sosial kultural only. |
| Teknis 90 / manajerial 25 / sosial kultural 20 | Same diktum | Match |
| 100 soal for Pengelola Umum Operasional | Diktum KEDUA PULUH TUJUH: "bagi jabatan Pengelola Umum Operasional … adalah 100 (seratus) butir soal, dengan rincian: a. … teknis sejumlah 45 …; b. … manajerial sejumlah 25 …; c. … sosial kultural sejumlah 20 …; dan d. wawancara sejumlah 10" | Match. **Caveat:** for PUO, teknis is **45** soal, not 90. |
| 120 menit | Diktum KETUJUH BELAS: "Seleksi kompetensi teknis, manajerial dan sosial kultural … dilaksanakan dalam durasi waktu 120 (seratus dua puluh) menit." | Match. The 120 minutes cover teknis, manajerial and sosial kultural; wawancara is separate. |
| Wawancara 10 menit | Diktum KEDELAPAN BELAS: "Wawancara … dilaksanakan dalam durasi waktu 10 (sepuluh) menit." | Match. Diktum KEDUA PULUH and KEDUA PULUH SATU give 150 + 15 menit for "pelamar penyandang disabilitas sensorik netra". |
| Max score 670 | Diktum KEDUA PULUH EMPAT: "Nilai kumulatif paling tinggi untuk seleksi kompetensi dan wawancara adalah 670 …: a. 450 … teknis; b. 180 … manajerial dan sosial kultural; dan c. 40 … wawancara." | Match |
| Max 445 for PUO | Diktum KEDUA PULUH DELAPAN: "bagi jabatan Pengelola Umum Operasional … adalah 445 …: a. 225 … teknis; b. 180 …; dan c. 40 … wawancara." | Match |
| No passing grade, ranking-based | Diktum KEDUA PULUH SEMBILAN: "Pelamar dinyatakan lulus seleksi jika berperingkat terbaik." KemenPANRB 23 Aug 2024: "dalam seleksi tidak menggunakan nilai ambang batas". | Match |

**Scoring.** Diktum KEDUA PULUH TIGA of 347/2024 (and of 349/2024):

- Teknis: "bobot jawaban benar bernilai 5 (lima) dan salah atau tidak menjawab bernilai 0 (nol)".
- Manajerial, sosial kultural and wawancara: "bobot jawaban benar paling rendah 1 (satu) dan nilai paling tinggi 4 (empat), serta tidak menjawab bernilai 0 (nol)".

**Difference for guru (KepmenPANRB 348/2024, Diktum KEDUA PULUH EMPAT huruf a).** Teknis for guru is scored "bobot jawaban benar paling rendah 1 (satu) dan nilai paling tinggi 5 (lima)", not 5/0. Counts (145), durations (120 + 10) and maxima (670) are the same.

- Diktum KEDUA PULUH TUJUH: holders of a linear sertifikat pendidik "mendapat nilai paling tinggi sebesar 100% … dari nilai paling tinggi Kompetensi Teknis".
- 347/2024 Diktum KEDUA PULUH LIMA has the same rule for JF guru di instansi pusat.

**Applicant scope (TA 2024 only).** 347/2024 Diktum KEDUA: the needs are "diperuntukkan bagi pelamar: a. eks Tenaga Honorer Kategori II (eks THK-II); atau b. tenaga non Aparatur Sipil Negara (tenaga non-ASN)." The 2024 format was a non-ASN round, not an open public round.

**Mismatches found: none in the numbers.** Three wording risks:

1. 145 and 100 include the 10 wawancara items.
2. PUO teknis is 45, not 90.
3. The "5/0" teknis scoring does not apply to guru (1–5 per answer).
